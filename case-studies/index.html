Common scoring model

A score is a discussion aid, not a claim of certification.

Each case uses five weighted criteria. A criterion scores 0 when absent or untested, 1 when materially incomplete, 2 when partly effective, 3 when usable with small gaps, and 4 when clearly evidenced and repeatable. The weighted total is converted to a percentage.

Scores describe the reviewed sample and scope only. They do not establish legal compliance, clinical validity, investment expertise, security certification or business outcomes.
