Privacy

What this site stores, and what it does not.

This page describes the current public portfolio at shafeeqahfrancis-gif.github.io/portfolio. It is not a claim that analytics are anonymous.

Enquiries

If you use the enquiry form and the save succeeds, the brief is stored so it can be reviewed. That record includes the contact details you entered, the service or role, description, timing, optional budget note and optional public link. Access is limited to authorised administrators. Ordinary visitors cannot read other people’s enquiries.

Opening WhatsApp does not send a message and does not by itself create a stored enquiry. If a save fails, the form keeps your answers and WhatsApp is offered only as a fallback.

No automated email or WhatsApp message is sent by this site.

Usage events

If you allow analytics events, the site may record page views and button events such as case-study views, downloads, LinkedIn clicks, WhatsApp clicks, enquiry starts, saved enquiries and resource-link clicks. Those events do not include form contents, names, phone numbers or emails. They are page and button counts, not unique visitors.

You can decline analytics events in the consent prompt. The enquiry form can still be used.

Contact details on this site

The public LinkedIn profile and WhatsApp number are published so you can make contact. Do not send health records, customer files or passwords through WhatsApp or the enquiry form.

Questions

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp if you want a stored brief corrected or removed.
