Case Escalation Workflow

Purpose: Give service teams a consistent way to assess urgency, assign ownership and monitor unresolved cases.

Triage: Classify the case by customer impact, compliance risk and time sensitivity. Route it to the correct owner with a target response time.

Control: Record each handoff, action and decision. Escalate overdue or high-risk cases and close only when the resolution evidence is complete.
