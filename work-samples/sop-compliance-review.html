SOP Compliance Review

Purpose: Verify whether a service process follows its documented procedure and create a usable record of exceptions.

Method: Review each required control, capture the evidence, mark compliant or non-compliant, grade the risk and assign a corrective action.

Output: A concise exception log that shows what happened, why it matters, who owns the action and when it must be resolved.
