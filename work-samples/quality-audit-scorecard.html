Service Quality Audit Scorecard

Purpose: Create a repeatable way to review service cases, record evidence and turn findings into coaching priorities.

Approach: The sample separates critical compliance checks from weighted quality criteria. It records the evidence reviewed, failure reason, corrective action, owner and due date.

Measures: Overall quality score, compliance pass rate, critical failures, repeat issue frequency and open actions.

Employer value: The framework makes results comparable and gives managers a clear route from audit finding to improvement action.
